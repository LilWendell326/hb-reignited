Ideas and plans

Roadmap

What we are working on next and what we would like to add. Plans can change, and some ideas will go to a server poll first.

We finish and test what is built before starting something new, and we keep changes small so the game still feels like Helbreath.

Next up

Bigger, better parties

  • Planned Parties of up to about 100.

    For gardens, events and big hunts. Exp is still shared evenly between members close by.

  • Planned Join from anywhere.

    Invite players who are on a different map.

  • Planned Quick invite.

    Ctrl+P on a name in the chat history sends a party invite.

  • Planned Auto-accept invites.

    An option in Settings, off by default.

  • Planned Map pings.

    Click the minimap to flash a marker for your party.

  • Planned A scrolling member list, and an option to hide party dots in big groups.

Planned

Guild quests

  • Planned Huge shared quests.

    The whole guild works on one goal together. No time limit, so small guilds just take longer.

  • Planned Four areas at once.

    One quest each for the farm, the hunting grounds and Promiseland, the mid game, and the end game, so every level can help.

  • Planned A Huntmaster.

    A member the Guildmaster chooses to pick the guild's quests.

  • Planned Rewards for the guild.

    Quests fill a shared guild pool for upgrades such as guild teleports. Every member's part is counted.

Planned

More to come

  • Planned Smoother movement.

    The next step for the look and feel, keeping the classic art.

  • Planned More of the Barracks Trial.

    Harder and end-game versions, a reward and a title.

  • Planned Heldenian.

    A full check of the Heldenian event.

  • Planned Spanish.

    One client with a language choice when it starts.
