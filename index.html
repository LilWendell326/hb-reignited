Helbreath Reignited

CLASSIC, REKINDLED

The Helbreath you remember: the same art, the same towns, the same war between Aresden and Elvine. We fix what was broken, smooth out what was rough, and add more to do with your friends.

Staying classic

What stays the same

  • The original art and look.

    Classic sprites, maps and windows. New windows are drawn to match the old ones.

  • The classic 800 by 600 play area.

    Widescreen only adds map scenery and your minimap, timer and quest tracker at the sides. Nobody sees more of the fight than anyone else.

  • No paid items.

    Nothing that adds power is for sale, and there is no paid currency.

  • Players have a say.

    Server polls in the City Hall let players vote on ideas and changes.
